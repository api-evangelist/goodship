The ROI comes from four places: awards built through optimization, gaps closed in weeks because every lane is compared continuously, cascade cost removed once you can see real cost per load, and analyst weeks recovered from manual reconciliation and reporting. The first three move the freight budget. The fourth moves headcount capacity.

Transportation networks don’t operate in a single mode, and transportation procurement software shouldn’t either. Today, we’re excited to announce that GoodShip now supports LTL procurement, giving shippers the ability to manage LTL alongside FTL and intermodal in one platform. Transportation teams can now manage LTL alongside truckload in GoodShip, bringing more of their procurement into one place with flexible RFPs and mini-bids, streamlined bid evaluation, and award scenario modeling.

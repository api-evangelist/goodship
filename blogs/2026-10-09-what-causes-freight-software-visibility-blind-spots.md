Your freight data lives in multiple places: your loads in one system, contracts in another, market benchmarks somewhere else entirely. When these data sources don't connect, you lose the ability to see what's happening across your network at the lane level. This gap is what creates freight software visibility blind spots: the hidden zones where cost signals and service trends go unnoticed until they become expensive problems.

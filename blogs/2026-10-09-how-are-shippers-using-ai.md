Shippers are using AI to run and analyze RFPs, model award scenarios before committing, benchmark freight rates against the market and their budget, monitor carrier performance, and ask questions about their own network in plain language. Taken together, those add up to the real shift: keeping the network continuously optimized instead of correcting it once a year at bid time. AI is not running these shippers' transportation departments.

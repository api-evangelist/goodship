AI can help freight brokers price smarter, understand their network more deeply, and make faster decisions, but only when the underlying data is in place. For most brokerages, the real barrier isn't AI adoption. It's fragmented data: pricing history in spreadsheets, carrier performance buried in a TMS, customer insights scattered across email threads.

The best pricing software for freight brokers is the platform that protects your margin per load, not the one that helps you quote fastest. Of course speed matters, but a fast quote at a losing price is just a faster way to lose money. In a market where the difference between growth and survival is a few points of margin, the best pricing software connects your historical costs, your network data, and live market context into every price you put in front of a customer.
